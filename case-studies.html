BNG worked examples: 4-home rural, 50-home greenfield, brownfield mixed-use

Three worked examples showing PEA, metric, on-site/off-site cost, S106 legal, and 30-year management for a typical SME scheme range.

Awaiting MCIEEM peer review · CIEEM (Chartered Institute of Ecology and Environmental Management)

Case A: 4-home rural infill, 0.6 ha, lowland modified grassland

  • PEA + UKHab baseline: £1,800
  • Metric output: 2.88 baseline BU; delta 3.17 BU after 10% uplift assuming no on-site retention.
  • On-site delivery via species-rich grassland creation: £28k incl 30-yr management.
  • Off-site purchase at medium distinctiveness: £82k (3.17 BU x £26k mid-rate).
  • Statutory credits (effective): £266k.
  • S106 legal: £3,500.
  • Chosen route: on-site. Total cost: £33,300.

Case B: 50-home greenfield, 2.4 ha, mixed habitat

  • PEA + UKHab + Phase 2 bat survey: £5,200
  • Metric output: 14.4 baseline BU; delta 15.84 BU after 10% uplift.
  • On-site delivery infeasible at scale; SUDS pond integration delivers 3 BU.
  • Off-site purchase: 12.84 BU at £32k mid-rate = £411k.
  • S106 legal: £6,200; 30-yr management bundled into off-site price.
  • Chosen route: hybrid on-site (3 BU) + off-site (12.84 BU). Total cost: £423k.

Case C: brownfield mixed-use, 1.1 ha, post-industrial habitat

  • PEA + invertebrate survey: £3,800 (post-industrial habitats can carry priority status).
  • Metric output: 6.6 baseline BU at medium distinctiveness; delta 7.26 BU.
  • On-site delivery via brownfield-targeted habitat creation: £95k.
  • Off-site purchase: £232k.
  • S106 legal: £4,100.
  • Chosen route: on-site. Total cost: £102,900. Brownfield exemption consultation may further reduce future cost.
