Trading-rule and spatial-risk note

Off-site units sourced inside the LPA carry the 1.0 spatial multiplier; neighbouring NCA discounts to 0.75; outside NCA discounts to 0.5. Combine with the like-for-like trading rule when triangulating compliant supply.
