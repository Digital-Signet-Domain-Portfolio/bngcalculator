Small Sites Metric (SSM): when it applies and what it returns

The Small Sites Metric (SSM) is Defra’s simplified metric for development that is not major development: 1 to 9 dwellings on a site of 1 hectare or less (or under 0.5 ha where the number of dwellings is unknown), or commercial floorspace under 1,000 square metres or a site under 1 hectare. It can only be used where no priority habitats, statutory protected sites or European protected species are present.

Eligibility

The SSM is for small sites, meaning development that is not major development: residential development of 1 to 9 dwellings on a site of 1 hectare or less, or under 0.5 ha where the number of dwellings is unknown; or commercial development creating less than 1,000 sqm of floorspace or on a site of less than 1 hectare. Minerals and waste development cannot use it. Even on a small site, the SSM is only available where every habitat on site is one the SSM includes, and no priority habitats, statutory protected sites or European protected species are present; otherwise the full statutory metric must be used. The full metric can always be used instead of the SSM.[S2]

Interaction with the 0.2 ha small-sites exemption (in force 6 August 2026)

A site of 0.2 ha or below that has no negative impact on onsite priority habitat is exempt from BNG under the 0.2 ha exemption, which applies to planning applications made on or after 6 August 2026; the SSM is therefore only material for the 0.2 to 1 ha band.[S4] A self-builder remodelling a 0.15 ha curtilage does not need either tool; a 0.6 ha infill on lowland modified grassland does.

Input set

  • Site boundary area in hectares.
  • Dominant baseline habitat (UKHab Level 3).
  • Condition is not surveyed: the SSM assigns baseline condition automatically, and post-development condition is picked from a set list, defaulting to the lowest unless evidence supports higher.
  • Post-development habitat composition.

Strategic significance is set per habitat parcel. Once a Local Nature Recovery Strategy has been published, baseline parcels in that area always score low, and only post-development habitat in a location the strategy maps for that measure can score high (1.15).[S2]

Feasibility cost build-up for a self-builder

A 0.4 ha self-build on modified grassland needs a baseline survey and an SSM output prepared by the ecologist. Modified grassland is a Tier A1 habitat on the statutory credit price list at £42,000 per credit, and 2 credits are needed per unit, so every unit bought as statutory credits costs £84,000. On-site creation and off-site units are priced by the ecologist’s plan and the habitat bank’s quote; neither has a published rate.[S3]

SSM is not a softer rulebook
The SSM simplifies the inputs, not the requirement: the 10% net gain still applies, and you should use one tool for the whole on-site calculation, not a mix of the SSM and the full metric.
