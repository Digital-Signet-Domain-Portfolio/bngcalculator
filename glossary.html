BNG glossary: plain-English definitions of every acronym

A concise BNG glossary; every term used elsewhere on this site is defined here. Use the cross-links to drill into the page that explains the concept in depth.

Awaiting MCIEEM peer review · CIEEM (Chartered Institute of Ecology and Environmental Management)
BU (Biodiversity Unit)
The output of the metric; sized by distinctiveness x condition x area x strategic significance, with risk multipliers on post-development.
LPA (Local Planning Authority)
The local authority responsible for determining the planning application; the BNG validation gate.
PEA (Preliminary Ecological Appraisal)
An ecologist’s initial survey of a site’s habitats and protected-species potential; see baseline survey cost for published prices.
UKHab
UK Habitat Classification; the underlying habitat taxonomy used by the metric.
SSM (Small Sites Metric)
Defra’s simplified metric for development that is not major development (for example 1 to 9 dwellings on 1 ha or less) with no priority habitats or protected sites on site; condition is assigned automatically.
SBM (Statutory Biodiversity Metric)
The full metric used for mandatory BNG; Defra’s calculation tool (version 1.1.1, November 2026) and user guide are its canonical implementation.
NSIP (Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project)
Projects consented under the Planning Act 2008; in scope of BNG from 2 November 2026.
NCA (National Character Area)
Natural England landscape unit; spatial risk multiplier bands are defined by NCA proximity.
LNRS (Local Nature Recovery Strategy)
Statutory mapping of priority areas for nature recovery; drives strategic significance multiplier.
Biodiversity Metric 4.0
The last pre-statutory version of the metric, published by Natural England. GOV.UK publishes a guide to transferring 4.0 data into the statutory tool.
SRM (Spatial Risk Multiplier)
The x2 doubling applied to statutory credits; the punitive backstop design feature.
HMMP (Habitat Management and Monitoring Plan)
The 30-year management and monitoring document required for off-site delivery.
