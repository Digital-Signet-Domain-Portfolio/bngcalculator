Cost per biodiversity unit (2026): statutory prices and off-site quotes

The only published biodiversity unit prices in England are Defra's statutory credits: £42,000 to £650,000 per credit, and 2 credits per unit, so £84,000 per unit at the cheapest tier (GOV.UK, updated 2 June 2026). Off-site units from habitat banks are priced by quote.

Biodiversity unit cost: the published figures

The only per-unit prices published for England are Defra’s statutory biodiversity credits, sold by Natural England as a last resort. Each tier is priced per credit, and a spatial risk multiplier means you buy 2 credits for every biodiversity unit. At the cheapest tier, A1 (£42,000 per credit), one unit therefore costs £84,000. The tier follows the specific habitat type, not just its distinctiveness band.[S3]

Off-site units: priced by quote

Off-site units bought from a habitat bank or landowner have no public rate card. GOV.UK states that statutory credit prices are not guideline prices for off-site units; they include a mark-up so they do not undercut the off-site market, and Defra reviews them every 6 months.[S3] GOV.UK’s guidance for developers says prices and payment terms should be agreed between you and the seller.[S18] The biodiversity gain sites register shows which sites are registered and what habitat they hold, which is where to start a search for compliant supply.[S5]

What changes an off-site quote

  • Habitat type and band. The trading rules require like-for-like replacement for high distinctiveness habitat, so a high-band loss narrows the sites that can supply you.[S1]
  • Location. Units from a site in the same local planning authority or national character area count in full; a neighbouring area applies a 0.75 spatial risk multiplier and anywhere further 0.5, so you buy more units to cover the same loss.[S1]
  • The 30-year commitment. The seller must manage and monitor the habitat for at least 30 years, and the quote carries that obligation.[S5]

Work out your own figure

The BNG calculator sizes the units your site needs, prices them as statutory credits from the GOV.UK list, and multiplies them by any off-site quote you enter, so you can compare the two routes side by side.

Quote-only operators
Environment Bank and Nattergal do not publish per-unit pricing; both quote per project, based on the planning authority, habitat composition, and contract structure.
