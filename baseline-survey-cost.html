BNG baseline survey cost: PEA and UKHab pricing (2026)

Most ecologists quote per site. One that publishes its prices, Brindle Green, lists a Preliminary Ecological Appraisal (PEA) at £500 plus VAT for small local sites and around £2,000 plus VAT for large, complex sites, with OS mapping (£50 to £300 plus VAT) and a data search (typically £300 plus VAT) charged separately (read 15 November 2026).

A published price list

Brindle Green, an ecological consultancy, publishes its PEA prices: £500 plus VAT for the survey and report on a small site local to its offices, and around £2,000 plus VAT for a large, complex site. Ordnance Survey mapping is charged separately at £50 to £300 plus VAT depending on site size, and an ecological data search, which planning authorities may ask for, is typically £300 plus VAT.[S23] These are one firm’s list prices, read on 15 November 2026, not a market average.

What a BNG baseline adds to a PEA

A PEA screens a site for habitats and protected species. A BNG submission also needs a UKHab habitat map, a condition assessment for each habitat parcel, and the statutory metric completed from them, so ask for those to be quoted alongside the PEA.[S1]

What drives the quote

  • Site size and how many separate habitat parcels need a condition assessment.
  • Habitat complexity: a single arable field is quicker to survey and write up than a mosaic of grassland, scrub and wetland.
  • Hedgerows and watercourses, which have their own modules in the metric.
  • Timing: condition depends on plants being identifiable, so a survey outside the growing season may need a return visit.
  • Travel from the ecologist’s base, which is why Brindle Green’s lower price applies to sites local to its offices.

What feeds the statutory metric tool

  • UKHab habitat polygons.
  • Condition score per polygon (poor to good, from the condition assessment sheets) with evidence.
  • Hedgerow and watercourse modules where applicable.
  • Strategic significance, checked against the Local Nature Recovery Strategy.

When Phase 2 species surveys are triggered

If the PEA finds potential for protected species such as great crested newt, bats, dormice or water vole, further species surveys follow. They are quoted separately from the BNG baseline and are seasonal, so they can set the programme as well as the cost.
