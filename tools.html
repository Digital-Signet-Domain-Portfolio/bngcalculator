BNG tools and operators

Profiles of the BNG tools, marketplaces and habitat bank operators covered on this site.

Awaiting MCIEEM peer review · CIEEM (Chartered Institute of Ecology and Environmental Management)
  • Environment Bank pricing: operator-owned habitat bank profile
    Profile and pricing for Environment Bank: Quote-only; no per-unit price published.
  • Joe's Blooms pricing: paid compliance SaaS for BNG
    Profile and pricing for Joe's Blooms: £95 inc VAT (Explore Tool per project); BNG Tool from £495 inc VAT.
  • Naturefy review: gated free BNG metric tool
    Profile and pricing for Naturefy: Free after signup (no per-project fee).
  • Gaia biodiversity marketplace: profile and pricing model
    Profile and pricing for Gaia: Marketplace; no per-unit price published openly.
  • BNG Finder review: free unit-search aggregator
    Profile and pricing for BNG Finder: Free search (no signup).
  • Nattergal biodiversity: landowner-led habitat bank profile
    Profile and pricing for Nattergal: Quote-only. Sites include Boothby Wildland (Lincolnshire) and Loch Ness (registered Scottish entity).
  • Biodiversity Units UK: off-site BNG unit broker
    Profile and pricing for Biodiversity Units UK: Brokered transaction; no per-unit price published.
  • Defra biodiversity metric download: JP039 v4.0 spreadsheet
    Profile and pricing for Defra Statutory Biodiversity Metric (JP039): Free Excel-based tool (Statutory Metric + Small Sites Metric).
